JOB VACANCY

Junior Producer, Harbour Lane Studio, Bristol
Full time, permanent
Salary: GBP 27,000 to GBP 30,000 a year

About us
Harbour Lane Studio is an independent photography and podcast studio working with brands and broadcasters across the UK.

The role
You will help plan and run studio shoots, book crews and equipment, and keep clients updated from first brief to final delivery.

What you'll do
- Coordinate bookings and call sheets.
- Look after budgets for small productions.
- Be the first point of contact for clients on shoot days.

What we're looking for
- At least one year of experience in production or events.
- Excellent organisation and communication skills.
- A full driving licence is helpful but not essential.

How to apply
Send your CV and a short cover letter through our careers page. Closing date: 31 October 2026.
