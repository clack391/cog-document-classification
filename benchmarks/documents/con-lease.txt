Landlord: Quayside Estates Ltd, whose registered office is at 1 Ferry Steps, Bristol XB1 2RT
Tenant: Harbour Lane Studio Ltd, whose registered office is at 3 Quay Street, Bristol XB1 4DB

Premises: the ground and first floors of 3 Quay Street, Bristol XB1 4DB

1. Term
The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for 5 years, from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2028.

2. Rent
2.1 The rent is GBP 32,000 a year, plus VAT.
2.2 The Tenant shall pay the rent in equal quarterly instalments in advance on the usual quarter days.

3. Deposit
The Tenant has paid a rent deposit of GBP 8,000, which the Landlord holds for the term.

4. Use
The Premises may be used only as a photography and recording studio with ancillary offices.

5. Repairs
The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good repair. The Landlord is responsible for the structure and the roof.

6. Break clause
The Tenant may end this Lease on 31 December 2026 by giving at least 6 months' written notice.

7. Governing law
This Lease is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
