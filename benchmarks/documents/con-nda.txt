MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into as of 14 August 2026 by and between Larkspur Analytics Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Larkspur"), and Fernhill Biotech Ltd, a company incorporated in England ("Fernhill").

WHEREAS the parties wish to explore a possible collaboration on laboratory data analysis (the "Purpose") and, in doing so, may disclose confidential information to each other;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, in any form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.
2. Obligations. The receiving party shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing party.
3. Exclusions. These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party.
4. Term. This Agreement remains in force for three (3) years from the date above.
5. Return of Materials. On request, the receiving party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information.
6. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
