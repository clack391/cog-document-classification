CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This agreement is dated 22 September 2026 and is between:

(1) Harbour Lane Studio Ltd, of 3 Quay Street, Bristol XB1 4DB (the "Studio"); and
(2) Maya Okafor, trading as Pixel & Pine Design, of 7 Orchard Mews, Bristol XB6 4QT (the "Designer").

1. Purpose
The Studio will share client briefs, unreleased artwork and pricing so that the Designer can work on client projects (the "Purpose").

2. Confidential information
Confidential information means everything the Studio shares for the Purpose, in any form, except information that is already public through no fault of the Designer.

3. Obligations
The Designer shall:
(a) use the confidential information only for the Purpose;
(b) not disclose it to anyone without the Studio's written consent;
(c) return or delete it when the Studio asks.

4. Duration
These obligations continue for 3 years after the last project the Designer works on.

5. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
