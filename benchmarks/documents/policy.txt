Travel and Expenses Policy
Version 3.1, effective 1 September 2026

1. Purpose
This policy explains what business travel and expenses the company pays for, and how to claim them.

2. Who it applies to
All employees and contractors who travel on company business.

3. Travel
3.1 Book standard-class rail travel at least 7 days ahead where possible.
3.2 Flights need prior approval from a director.
3.3 Mileage in a personal car is paid at 45p per mile.

4. Accommodation
Hotel stays are covered up to GBP 130 per night in London and GBP 95 elsewhere, including breakfast.

5. Meals
Reasonable meal costs are covered when travelling, up to GBP 30 per day.

6. Claims
Submit claims within 30 days, with receipts, through the expenses system. Claims without receipts may be refused.

7. Questions
Ask the operations team.
