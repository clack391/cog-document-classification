Harbour Lane Studio - Quarterly Operations Report
Q3 2026 (July to September)

1. Summary
Studio utilisation rose to 78% this quarter, up from 71% in Q2. Two new long-term clients signed, and the photography suite was refurbished on budget.

2. Bookings
- Total bookings: 214 (Q2: 187)
- Average booking length: 5.5 hours
- Cancellation rate: 4% (Q2: 6%)

3. Finance overview
Revenue was GBP 148,200 against a target of GBP 140,000. Equipment spending was GBP 12,450, mostly lighting upgrades.

4. People
One junior producer joined in August. Staff turnover remained at zero.

5. Priorities for Q4
- Launch online booking for the podcast room.
- Review insurance cover before renewal in December.
- Reduce energy use in the main studio by 10%.

Prepared by the operations team, 7 October 2026.
