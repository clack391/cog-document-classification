TERM LOAN FACILITY AGREEMENT

Dated: 5 May 2026

Between:
(1) Meridian Bank plc (the "Lender"); and
(2) Harbour Lane Studio Ltd (the "Borrower").

1. The facility
The Lender makes available to the Borrower a term loan of GBP 50,000 (the "Loan").

2. Purpose
The Borrower shall use the Loan only to buy studio lighting, cameras and recording equipment.

3. Interest
Interest is charged at 2.9% a year above the Lender's base rate, calculated daily and paid monthly.

4. Repayment
The Borrower shall repay the Loan in 48 equal monthly instalments, the first falling due one month after drawdown.

5. Conditions
The Lender is not obliged to advance the Loan until it has received a board resolution of the Borrower approving this Agreement.

6. Events of default
If the Borrower fails to pay any amount when due, or becomes insolvent, the Lender may demand immediate repayment of everything outstanding.

7. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
