DEED OF VARIATION AND EXTENSION OF LEASE

Date: 1 October 2026

Parties:
(1) Quayside Estates Ltd (the "Landlord")
(2) Harbour Lane Studio Ltd (the "Tenant")

Background
(A) The Landlord let 3 Quay Street, Bristol XB1 4DB to the Tenant by a lease dated 15 December 2023 (the "Lease"), for a term ending on 31 December 2028.
(B) The parties have agreed to extend the term and change the rent from the end of the current term.

It is agreed:

1. Extension
The term of the Lease is extended to 31 December 2031.

2. Rent
From 1 January 2029 the rent is GBP 34,500 a year, plus VAT, payable as set out in the Lease.

3. Break clause
The Tenant's break right on 31 December 2026 is removed. A new break right applies on 31 December 2029, on at least 6 months' written notice.

4. Everything else unchanged
In all other respects the Lease continues in full force.

5. Governing law
This deed is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
