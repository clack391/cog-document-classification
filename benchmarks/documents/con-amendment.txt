AMENDMENT No. 1 TO THE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment is made on 25 September 2026 between:

(1) Brightline IT Services Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales under number 08841276 ("Supplier"); and
(2) Harbour Lane Studio Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales under number 11902345 ("Customer").

BACKGROUND
(A) The Parties entered into a Master Services Agreement dated 2 June 2026 (the "Agreement").
(B) The Parties wish to add a cloud backup service and extend the initial term of the Agreement.

THE PARTIES AGREE as follows:

1. Interpretation
Words defined in the Agreement have the same meaning in this Amendment.

2. Additional service
2.1 With effect from 1 October 2026, Schedule 1 of the Agreement is amended to add "Cloud backup for up to 20 devices".
2.2 Clause 3.1 of the Agreement is amended so that the monthly fee is GBP 970.00 plus VAT.

3. Extension of term
Clause 2.1 of the Agreement is amended so that the initial term is 36 months instead of 24 months.

4. Everything else unchanged
Except as set out in this Amendment, the Agreement remains in full force and effect.

5. Governing law
This Amendment is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
