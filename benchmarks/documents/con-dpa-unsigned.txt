DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT
Version for signature, 12 October 2026

This Agreement is between Harbour Lane Studio Ltd (the "Controller") and Ledgerly Software Ltd (the "Processor"). It forms part of the Ledgerly Plus subscription between them.

1. Subject matter
The Processor processes personal data only to provide the Ledgerly Plus service, and only on the Controller's documented instructions.

2. Types of data
Names and contact details of the Controller's customers and suppliers, and payroll details of the Controller's staff.

3. Security
The Processor shall keep appropriate technical and organisational measures in place, including encryption of data at rest and in transit.

4. Sub-processors
The Processor shall not appoint a new sub-processor without giving the Controller 30 days' notice and a chance to object.

5. Personal data breaches
The Processor shall notify the Controller of a personal data breach without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours of becoming aware of it.

6. End of processing
When the subscription ends, the Processor shall delete or return all personal data, as the Controller chooses.

7. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
